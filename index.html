02 — What it costs

The information is not missing. Nothing decides what it means.

  1. The public record

    All of it true. None of it written together.

  2. Many fragments

    Nothing complete, nothing current, nothing ordered by importance.

  3. Different readers

    A client, a board, a journalist, a referral. Each one lands somewhere else.

  4. No clear source of truth

    A search engine does not rank the truth. It ranks what has a page.

You spent a career earning a reputation. The internet is assembling it without you.
